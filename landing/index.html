Common questions

Is my bank data secure?

Yes. Bank connections are powered by Plaid, the same technology used by Venmo, Coinbase, and thousands of other apps. We never see or store your bank login credentials. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

How accurate are the tax estimates?

FlowLedger uses the current IRS tax brackets, self-employment tax rate (15.3%), the SE tax deduction, and all 50 state income tax rates. Estimates are typically within 5% of actual taxes owed. Always verify with a CPA before paying.

What if I have multiple income streams?

FlowLedger handles multiple bank accounts and income sources. All income is aggregated for tax estimation, and you can categorize each transaction individually.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from within the app or your App Store account settings. Your subscription continues until the end of the paid period — no partial refunds.

Does it work for S-Corp or LLC owners?

FlowLedger is optimized for sole proprietors and single-member LLCs filing Schedule C. S-Corp payroll and multi-member partnerships are not currently supported.
